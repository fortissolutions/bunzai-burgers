● How We Got Here

Four Chapters. One Obsession.

01

The Obsession

It started with one question: why do most burgers taste the same? Hundreds of hours of testing griddle temperatures, fat ratios and buns followed.

02

The Smash

We landed on the method: loose Angus balls, 600°F cast iron, one hard press. Lacy, caramelized edges every single time.

03

Muwaileh

Our flagship opened on University City Road in Sharjah — dine-in, takeaway and a late-night drive-thru for the cravings that hit after midnight.

04

Today

Fifty thousand happy customers later, every patty is still smashed to order the moment you ask for it. No shortcuts, no heat lamps.
