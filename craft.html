Artisan Sourdough Bun

48-hr slow fermented, toasted with clarified butter to seal in moisture without getting soggy.

Our crown starts two days before your order. The dough ferments slowly for 48 hours, building a gentle sourdough tang and a soft, springy crumb that can stand up to a juicy smash.

Every bun is split and toasted face-down in clarified butter. That golden, sealed surface is what keeps the sauce and juices on the burger and off your hands — no soggy tops, ever.

Aged Cheddar & Smoked Aioli

Triple melted sharp Wisconsin cheddar with oak-smoked drizzle and creamy emulsified pepper.

Three slices of sharp aged cheddar go on while the patty is still on the iron, so they melt straight into the crust instead of sitting on top of it.

Then comes the aioli: emulsified in-house with cracked pepper and finished with an oak-smoked drizzle that adds depth without overpowering the beef.

100% Prime Angus Beef

Hand-smashed on 600°F cast iron for ultra-crispy caramelized lace edges and maximum savory crust.

We use 100% prime Angus with a fat ratio tuned over hundreds of test smashes. It is portioned into loose balls and never pressed ahead of time.

The moment you order, each ball hits 600°F cast iron and is smashed flat in one hard press. The thin edges turn into crispy, caramelized lace while the centre stays juicy.

Farm-Fresh Toppings

Crisp heirloom sliced tomatoes, chilled crunchy dill pickles & hydroponic butter lettuce leaf.

Toppings are there for contrast: cool, crunchy and bright against the hot, rich beef and cheese.

Heirloom tomatoes are sliced every morning, dill pickles are kept chilled for maximum snap, and butter lettuce leaves are washed and dried so they stay crisp under the patty.

Final Steam & Smash

Pressed, married under a smoking steel dome for 20 seconds, and served piping hot immediately.

Once stacked, the whole burger goes under a steel dome with a splash of water for 20 seconds. The steam softens the bun, finishes the cheese melt and marries every layer together.

It then goes straight to you — no heat lamps, no waiting on a pass. That is the only way a smash burger should be eaten.
